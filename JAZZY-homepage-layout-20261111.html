LATEST UPDATE / BUILD 20261111.1

Let the kick lead.

Set the sidechain from an internal kick, a chosen track, incoming USB audio or a specific MIDI note. With Kick Link, a kick on RHYTHMER can duck a mix on JAZZY, and a JAZZY kick can drive RHYTHMER the same way.

  • More control over the envelope. Adjust attack, release and amount. MASTER’s DUCK and DUCK ENV’s AMT share one setting.
  • USB MIDI Host. Connect class-compliant MIDI gear, or link two FM-1s with one in Host mode and the other in Device mode.
  • 31 refreshed engine animations. Each engine has its own pixel backdrop. TRIO’s bird chases an insect while the note sounds, then settles on release. ENV, LFO and FX visuals were also refreshed.
  • CPU and mix headroom. The renderer reuses compact graphics and the existing redraw limits. Earlier attack, chord and mixer fixes carry forward to reduce clicks and clipping.
